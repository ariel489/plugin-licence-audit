How to

Move a licence to hello@ or dtclients@

  1. Log in to the vendor account that holds it. For Brett's accounts, ask Brett to log in or change the email. For Sandeep's, use a password reset sent to sandeep@ if the mailbox still exists, or ask the vendor's support to move the licence (give the site address and order date).
  2. Change the account email to hello@, or dtclients@ for anything billed, and put DT's card or PayPal on it. If the vendor will not change the email, buy the licence on hello@ and cancel the old one before it renews.
  3. On each site, enter the new key where the vendor guide says, and check that an update is offered or the licence shows as active.
  4. Deactivate the old key in the old account so its seat is freed.
  5. Add the licence, account and renewal date to the client profile.

Remove a paid plugin that is not used

Removing a plugin is a stop-and-ask change in CLAUDE.md: say what will change, what could break and how to undo it. Sites not on a maintenance plan need the client's OK first.

  1. Check the second-check result in the register. Anything marked "in use" stays.
  2. Back up: site.sh <key> backup-db, or a Cloudways backup.
  3. Free the seat: open the plugin's licence screen and deactivate the licence before the plugin.
  4. Deactivate the plugin, then check the pages that used it with site.sh <key> verify, logged out, on desktop and phone.
  5. Delete it under Plugins. The backup is the undo.
  6. At the vendor, turn off auto-renew, but only once no other site uses the same licence.
  7. Write the task record and a History line in the profile.

Undo: reinstall the zip from the vendor account and reactivate the licence; restore the database backup if settings were lost.

Update a premium plugin or theme

  1. Make sure the licence is active on the site (the plugin's licence screen). Without it, no update appears.
  2. Checkout, payment and shipping plugins: update the staging copy first and place a test order.
  3. Update from Dashboard > Updates. If nothing appears, download the zip from the vendor account and upload it: Plugins > Add New Plugin > Upload Plugin > Replace current with uploaded.
  4. Purge the caches and check the pages that use it, logged out.
